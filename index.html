Welcome to my blog!

This website is for my fellow picky eaters. The only thing that soothes my stomach is pizza, candy, chicken nuggets, and french fries! Throughout this blog I will tell you the best places to take the picky eaters in your life. We don't mean to embarrass you, we just love chicken nuggets.
