Little Caesars

5/5 Nuggets

Little Caesars is my go to for pizza. 5/5 nuggets. It is not nearly as greasy as other pizza places like Pizza Hut. They are also good for leftovers the next day. If Little Caesars ever starts a frozen pizza line, I will be first in line at Walmart. Little Caesers is so good it makes me feel content like the fish on the right. There is no other fast chain pizza place as good as Little Caesers in my opinion.
