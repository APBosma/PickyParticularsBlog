Little Caesars

5/5 Nuggets

Little Caesars is my go to for pizza. 5/5 nuggets. It is not nearly as greasy as other pizza places like Pizza Hut. They are also good for leftovers the next day. If Little Caesars ever starts a frozen pizza line, I will be first in line at Walmart.
